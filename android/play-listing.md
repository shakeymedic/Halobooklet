## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
HALO QRH is a quick reference handbook for High Acuity, Low Occurrence emergency procedures in adults. It is written for trained doctors, nurses and paramedics who may need to perform a rare, time-critical procedure and want a clear checklist to follow.

Each procedure has two parts:
• Prepare: the walkthrough to read while the team gets ready.
• Action: the steps to follow while doing it, with labelled diagrams for most procedures.

Procedures in this version (v2.9):
• Resuscitative hysterotomy
• Emergency breech delivery
• Resuscitative thoracotomy
• Escharotomy (burns)
• Open chest drain
• Pericardiocentesis
• External pacing
• Subclavian central line
• Lateral canthotomy
• Severe maxillofacial bleed
• LAST (local anaesthetic systemic toxicity, lipid rescue)
• Surgical airway (eFONA)

What the app does:
• Works with no signal: everything is stored on the phone.
• Search the procedures by name.
• Tick off steps as you go.
• Download the full printable PDF handbook to your phone.
• Keeps the screen on while the app is open.

Who it is for: trained healthcare professionals caring for patients aged 16 and over. It is not for children's emergencies.

Important: HALO QRH is a cognitive aid and teaching resource. It does not replace training, local policy or clinical judgement, and guidelines change. It is not a medical device. The content draws on published UK guidance (for example DAS, RCEM, FPHC and Resuscitation Council UK), but those organisations have not produced or endorsed this app.

The app has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking, and it collects no personal data.

Published by West Midlands Evidence Based Emergency Medicine Ltd (EM Evidence).
```
